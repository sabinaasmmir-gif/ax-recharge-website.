About AX RECHARGE

Built for straightforward utility payments and honest disclosure

AX RECHARGE is designed to bring mobile, DTH, and household bill workflows together in a fast, uncluttered web interface. Until official provider agreements and technical integrations are finalized, this website operates strictly as an informational portal.

01. Provider-Backed Integration First

No live recharge or utility bill payment will be accepted until authorized payment gateways, telecom aggregators, or Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) operating units are formally connected and verified.

02. Zero Credential Collection

AX RECHARGE never requests or stores UPI PINs, banking passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), CVVs, or card numbers. Future checkout flows will redirect strictly through authorized payment rails.

03. Transparent Business Identity

We publish clear placeholders wherever legal registration, tax identifiers, or physical office records are pending finalization, rather than displaying unverified numbers or fabricated testimonials.

Five core recharge and bill payment modules

01 · Mobile Recharge

Prepaid Mobile Recharge

Designed for quick 10-digit prepaid number lookup, telecom circle identification, and transparent plan comparison across data, talktime, and validity packs once authorized telecom APIs are connected.

02 · DTH Recharge

Direct-to-Home (DTH) Top-Ups

Planned support for monthly and annual DTH subscription renewals using your Subscriber ID or Registered Mobile Number, with clear validity summaries before checkout.

Required input: Subscriber / Customer ID · Status: Under Development
03 · Electricity Bills

Electricity Bill Payments

Planned integration for state and private power distribution boards to fetch exact billed amounts, consumer names, and due dates prior to confirmation.

Input: Consumer / CA Number · Status: Under Development
